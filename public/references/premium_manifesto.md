# THE PREMIUM SHOP MANIFESTO
**Goal:** Generate fully original, highly aesthetic, luxury-grade e-commerce shops. NO TEMPLATES. NO GENERIC CONTENT.

## ✅ THE 5 PILLARS OF EXCELLENCE

### 1. High-End Design & Visual Language
-   **Color Palette:** Thoughtful, consistent, and harmonious. Can be colorful if the brand demands it, but must be *stylish*, not loud.
-   **Typography:** Elegant hierarchy. Use Serif or Premium Sans-Serif. Sufficient whitespace.
-   **Imagery:** Professional grade. Lifestyle-focused. No pixelated or generic stock clichés.
-   **Layout:** "Minimalist & Calm" or "Atmospheric & Unique".
-   **Edge-to-Edge Philosophy:** Product grids should default to **gap-0** and full width. No padding between items. We want a "Magazine" look, not a "Card" look.

### 2. UX & Performance
-   **Navigation:** Intuitive but elegant.
-   **Interactions:** **NO GENERIC ZOOMS**. Do not use `hover:scale-105`. It looks cheap. Use **Second Image Reveal**, **Opacity Shift**, or **Underlines**.
-   **Speed:** Fast. Use lazy-loading concepts.
-   **Responsiveness:** Flawless on Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.

### 3. Storytelling & Brand Identity
-   **Identity:** Every shop needs a fictional *Soul* (History, Values, Tone).
-   **Copywriting:** High-class, emotional, distinct. No "Welcome to our shop". Use "Heritage", "Craftsmanship", "The Edit".
-   **Product Story:** Don't just list specs. Tell *how* the product fits into a lifestyle.

### 4. Originality & Custom Layouts (NO TEMPLATES)
-   **Custom DNA:** Every layout must be invented for *this* specific brand.
-   **Micro-Interactions:** Subtle, smooth animations. No gimmicks.
-   **Consistency:** The visual language must be unbreakable across all 13 pages.

### 5. Trust & Service
-   **Trust Signals:** Clear Shipping, Returns, Contact info.
-   **Concierge Feel:** Use language that implies service ("Concierge", "Assistance").

---

## ❌ THE "RED LINES" (STRICTLY FORBIDDEN)

-   **NO COPYING:** Do not clone existing brands 1:1. Remix and Interpret only.
-   **NO TEMPLATES:** If it looks like a $50 WP Theme, it is a failure.
-   **NO BAD COLORS:** No neon greens on red. No "Default Blue".
-   **NO GIMMICKS:** No aggressive popups. No spinning logos.
-   **NO GENERIC TEXT:** "Lorem Ipsum" or "Best Products" is forbidden.
-   **NO INCONSISTENCY:** Fonts must not change randomly.
